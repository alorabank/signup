Alora Bank — Account Agreement

By accepting this agreement you open an account with Alora Bank and agree to the terms below.

1. Account ownership

Your account is opened in the legal name you provided. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for all activity conducted through your account.

2. Deposits

Deposits may be made through the channels provided by the bank. All deposits are subject to verification and approval before funds are made available in your account.

3. Withdrawals and transfers

Withdrawal requests, including transfers to other Alora Bank customers or to external accounts, are subject to review and approval. Approved withdrawals are processed to the destination you specified.

4. Accurate information

You agree that the personal information provided during account opening is true and complete, and you will update it promptly if it changes.

5. Electronic communications

You consent to receive account communications, notices, and statements electronically.

6. Fees

Any applicable fees will be disclosed to you before they are charged. You authorize the bank to deduct disclosed fees from your account.

7. Privacy

Your personal information is collected and used for the operation of your account and as required by law. It is not sold to third parties.

8. Errors and unauthorized activity

Notify the bank promptly if you notice an error or suspect unauthorized activity on your account so it can be investigated.

9. Amendments

The bank may amend this agreement with reasonable notice. Continued use of your account after amendments take effect constitutes acceptance.

10. Account closure

You may request closure of your account at any time. The bank may close an account in accordance with this agreement and applicable law.
